---
title: "iOS performance improvements and Safari reminders"
---

[Saver 1.3.2 for iPhone and iPad](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6766563672) includes performance improvements.

The [Saver browser extension](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6771103161) also gained support for scheduling reminders on your phone in version 1.1, released on September 1. The [browser extension guide](https://docs.saver-app.com/browser-extension) explains how to sign in and save a todo from Safari.
